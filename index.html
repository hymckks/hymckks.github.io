Research

Clean-Label Backdoor Attacks in Personalized FLOct 2025 – Feb 2026
  • Studied clean-label backdoor attacks in device–edge personalized FL under a realistic threat model: a single attacker holding only target-class data.
  • Proposed the Target-Class Trigger attack, using a generative model to synthesize triggers from target-class features without label manipulation.
  • Evaluated on CIFAR-10 with FedAvg, Ditto, FedRep and pFedMe (ConvNet, ResNet-18): 68%–80% ASR at a 5% poisoning rate with negligible clean-accuracy loss.
Privacy-Preserving DBSCAN Clustering with FHEFeb 2022 – May 2022
Undergraduate thesis · code
  • Designed a privacy-preserving DBSCAN scheme based on fully homomorphic encryption for outsourced data analytics.
  • Used CKKS ciphertext packing (SIMD) to parallelize encrypted distance computation; implemented in C++ with HElib.
